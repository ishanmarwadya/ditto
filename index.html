Five rules that make trust structural

Not a promise. An architecture, with a control behind every rule.

1 Broker licence, not agent
Ditto acts for you by law, with access to every insurer, not just the nine a Corporate Agent licence allows.
2 Commission-blind pay
Advisors earn the same amount whichever policy you choose, so there's nothing to push.
3 No paid placement
Shortlists are ranked by the Claims Index, built from real, paid-out outcomes, never by who pays Ditto the most.
4 Earnings shown on every option
Every recommendation shows exactly what Ditto makes from it, side by side with the alternatives.
5 Annual independent audit
An outside auditor checks the numbers behind these rules and Ditto publishes the keep-rate every year.
